# AIOM Prose Style Guide v1: RETIRED

Superseded by `AIOM_Prose_Standard_v2.0.md` on 2026-08-19, together with
`AIOM_Voice_and_Craft_v1.md`. The voice is now called Concrete Management Prose and
it is defined in one place.

**Why it was retired rather than amended.** Its sentence-level rules were sound and
several survive verbatim in the new standard, but Chapter 1 was drafted while this
file was on `main` and still arrived in the wrong voice. Three things were missing:
a generative pattern telling a drafter what to write next, a ban on abstract
openings, and ordinary business language as the default rather than a register
named "magisterial". A fourth problem was structural: the prose standard was split
across two files plus CLAUDE.md plus Consolidated Spec B.2, and a rule that lives in
four places is a rule that disagrees with itself in two of them.
