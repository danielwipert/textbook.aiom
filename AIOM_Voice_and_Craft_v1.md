# AIOM Voice and Craft Standard v1: RETIRED

Superseded by `AIOM_Prose_Standard_v2.0.md` on 2026-08-19, together with
`AIOM_Prose_Style_Guide_v1.md`.

**What survives.** The six craft criteria remain the Stage 4 judgment instrument and
keep their numbers, so Chapter 1's record stays readable. They are restated in
section 26 of the new standard, where **C3 becomes "claim first"** to match the
core principle, **C6's guard now watches both directions** rather than only the
populist one, and **C7 is added** for business reality first, which nothing graded
before and which is the failure the rejected Chapter 1 draft committed most often.

**What was retired.** The four journalism exemplars, Lewis, Lardner, the Financial
Times and The New Yorker, are no longer the models for this voice. They taught
texture, and an MBA textbook on AI management needs managerial clarity. What they
contributed survives inside the criteria themselves. **The exemplar of the voice is
now the locked Chapter 1**, which is the book's own prose in the target register,
and the baseline band in section 27 of the new standard is measured from it.

The full prior text, including the exemplar analysis and the original band, is in
git history at `3cff85f`.
