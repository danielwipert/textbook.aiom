# Fact Check: AI Operations Management, Chapter 3

**WEB ACCESS: yes**

## A-1. PARTLY SUPPORTED [C1]

**Sentence:** “In February 2026, the FinOps Foundation changed its mission statement. For years, it had described its purpose as advancing the people who manage cloud value. The new statement replaced one word: the Foundation now advances the people who manage technology value.”

**Checked against:** “A One Word Change: How the FinOps Community Made Our Mission Evolution Inevitable,” FinOps Foundation, https://www.finops.org/insights/mission-update/, read October 13, 2026.

**Source says:** “Today, I’m announcing that the FinOps Foundation has officially updated our mission” and “advancing the people who manage the value of cloud” to “advancing the people who manage the value of technology.”

**Finding:** The February 2026 date and one word substitution are verified. The chapter omits “the value of” from both mission statements, so it does not reproduce the wording accurately.

**Proposed remedy:** “In February 2026, the FinOps Foundation updated its mission statement. For years, it had described its purpose as advancing the people who manage the value of cloud. The new statement replaced one word: the Foundation now advances the people who manage the value of technology.”

## A-2. VERIFIED [C2]

**Sentence:** “The 2026 survey reached 1,192 respondents responsible for more than $83 billion in annual cloud spending. … Ninety-eight percent of respondents now managed AI spending, up from 31 percent two years earlier.”

**Finding:** The respondent count, spending figure, and 98 percent figure are verified. The source expressly identifies 31 percent as the 2024 figure for the same measure, managing AI spend.

**Proposed remedy:** none

## A-3. PARTLY SUPPORTED [C2]

## A-6. PARTLY SUPPORTED [C3]

**Sentence:** “FinOps was built to address a specific problem: cloud computing let engineers buy capacity with a few lines of code, but the bills arrived after they had used it. The discipline developed to make engineering, finance, and business teams jointly accountable for that spending.”

**Checked against:** “2024 Changes to the Definition of Cloud FinOps,” FinOps Foundation, https://www.finops.org/insights/changes-to-finops-definition/, read October 13, 2026; “What is FinOps?,” FinOps Foundation, https://www.finops.org/introduction/what-is-finops/, read October 13, 2026.

**Source says:** “Variable consumption tracked after the fact” and “creates financial accountability through collaboration between engineering, finance, and business teams.”

**Finding:** The collaboration and accountability claim is verified. The historical source supports variable cloud consumption measured after use, but does not support the specific “few lines of code” formulation.

**Proposed remedy:** “FinOps developed around cloud’s distinctive cost and usage challenges, including variable consumption tracked after the fact. The discipline creates financial accountability through collaboration among engineering, finance, and business teams.”

## A-8. PARTLY SUPPORTED [C12]
