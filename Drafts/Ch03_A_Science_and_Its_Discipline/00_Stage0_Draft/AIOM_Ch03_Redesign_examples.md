## Caveats already visible

- Several survey figures come from vendors that sell cost-management products,
  and most were found only in secondary coverage. Each must be read in its
  original release.
- The JPMorgan figures are executive estimates with inconsistent dates across
  sources; the investor-day materials are the primary to find.
- Survey percentages are frequency claims: they are sourced here, so they
  satisfy standing rule 2 only once the fact check confirms them.
