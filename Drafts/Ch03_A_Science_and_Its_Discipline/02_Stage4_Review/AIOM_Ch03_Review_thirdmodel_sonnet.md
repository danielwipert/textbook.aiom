## Section A: Structure
The six slots are present and the outline is largely honoured, but competency C4 is only partly delivered. The worked chain stops at lemma level, and the chapter itself concedes that a reader cannot go below it without supplied propositions. The trace is load-bearing, not decorative: the authority condition surfaced at Step 4 is a real finding. Two accounts of the THM-004 conditions conflict, though. The borders slot cannot be judged because the table body is absent from Document 1. Nothing front-runs a later reveal beyond a small teaser about Part III ordering.

## Section B: Teaching
A sceptical reader will accept the mechanics of the trace but may stall on whether the exercise shows anything non-obvious. The sample propositions are near-tautologies and the falsifying case reads as close to definitional, so the word-games objection is answered in prose while the trace partly feeds it. The registry vocabulary arrives in one dense paragraph before any example. THM-004 is restated three times (3.2 and 3.3, the craft section, P1 and P2), which is heavy for a busy reader. The paragraph I would cut first is the "One further limit" tail of the non-claims paragraph in 3.3. The place most in need of a concrete example is the falsifying case.

## Section C: Voice and craft
The prose is mostly claim-first and well paced, and the opening case and 3.1 show the register at its best. Weakness concentrates in three places: the five-function previews (3.4), the borders (3.5), and paragraph endings that are cross-references or vague closers. The chapter leans on one anonymous practitioner quotation to carry the FinOps border, which strains the neutrality guard. A few aphorisms stand in for explanation. Each criterion has at least one row below, with the strongest passage named in the "What is wrong" cell.
