## Summary

1. C4 is taught but not fully deliverable: the reader is never told where the registry is (R-2), the worked lemma trace models only half of step 4 (R-3), and conditions versus scope and the falsifying case leave stall points at the centre of the skill (R-21, R-22).
2. The chapter breaks its own fidelity rule. Restatements turn LEM-020's and LEM-002's "or" into "and", and LEM-016's "may" into "have multiplied" (R-4, R-5). In a chapter that teaches "adding none", this is the most damaging defect.
3. The opening case overreaches its Grade C source: one quoted practitioner becomes what all practitioners report, and motive and causation are asserted without a source (R-40 to R-42). The cut "most organizations on earth" claim has returned in other words (R-1).
4. The Ch2 promises are paid by assertion, not by argument: the third-flow link (R-7) and "resolves the category error" (R-26). The "no three flows" non-claim is presented as the registry's when it is the book's (R-6).
5. Structural borders and apparatus: the deployment border collides with sourcing (R-11), P2 asks about contextual dependencies the chapter never taught (R-17), and signposting and aphorisms recur at section openings and closes (R-13, R-36, R-39).
