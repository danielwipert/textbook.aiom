# Scoped review: Chapter 3

## Section A: Structure

The 35 changed passages preserve the chapter’s purpose: distinguish the science from the discipline, teach registry literacy, preview the functions, and leave the Founding Questions unanswered. The dated registry counts remain in their designated box, and the Klarna case preserves Chapter 6’s later reveal. Two passages repeat material immediately beside them. The narrowed FinOps table entry is a deliberate limitation, although it no longer supplies a categorical answer to its column heading.

## Section B: Teaching

The principal teaching seam concerns what a dependency’s scope limits: its support for a theorem, rather than automatically the theorem’s coverage. The flat-fee example also shifts from a deployment as the falsifying case to a contract as the case. Elsewhere, the edits generally improve the distinction between a conditional claim, its recorded exclusions, and the book’s additional requirements of practice. The remaining findings concern repetition and presentation, not the settled accuracy rulings.

## Section C: Voice and craft

C1, C2, and C3 hold across the changed passages when read in context; their closest cases are recorded as deliberate choices. C4, C5, C6, and C7 have local defects involving an overloaded sentence, a trailing qualification, indirect wording, and a conceptual opening where the business mechanism is available. Necessary qualifications should remain explicit, with their placement improved. No finding below changes a formal statement or reopens sourcing.
