Source says: "The rideshare giant is limiting all employees to $1,500 in monthly
token spending per AI coding tool, an Uber spokesperson said in response to a
Bloomberg News inquiry." and "Every employee has a dashboard where they can
track their usage across various tools. The company has also implemented a
process by which individuals can seek permission to exceed their normal cap."

Proposed wording:
"In June 2026, Bloomberg News reported that Uber had set usage caps on some AI
tools after exhausting its AI budget earlier in the year. An Uber spokesperson
confirmed that all employees were limited to $1,500 a month in token spending
per AI coding tool. The limits applied only to agentic coding software, such as
Cursor and Claude Code. Bloomberg also reported that every employee had a
dashboard showing their own use across tools and could request permission to
exceed the cap."

## R-1: CANNOT CONFIRM (pending S4 and S8)

The article shows controls existed by June; it does not give their start date
or show whether any control changed spending before the budget ran out.

Source says: "The limits, which have been instituted in recent months..."

Bloomberg frames the caps as a response to the overrun, but "recent months" does
not establish that every control began after exhaustion. Do not turn an
unspecified date into a definite sequence.

Second point, on the clause about returns: Bloomberg reports that Uber
attributed slower planned hiring to AI's benefits, while its chief operating
officer questioned the link to more consumer features. Difficulty measuring
feature gains does not establish the absence of every form of return.

Proposed wording: hold until S4 and S8 are checked.
