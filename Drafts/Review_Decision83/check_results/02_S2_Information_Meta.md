"The company said" is fair, but "the memo said" is clearer. The leaderboard
figures come from The Information's own reporting on a dashboard copy it
reviewed, not from the memo or a Meta spokesperson. The 30-day period attaches
to 60.2 trillion only; no period is given for 73.7 trillion. The three quoted
fragments are otherwise exact.

Source says: "A copy of the dashboard reviewed by The Information in April
showed that employees had burned through 60.2 trillion tokens in a 30-day
period. Usage later climbed to 73.7 trillion tokens before the leaderboard was
taken down."

Proposed wording:
"In June 2026, Meta told employees in a memo that "individuals and teams have
limited visibility into and control over how they use AI." The memo said the
company was building an internal platform to track employee AI use and spending
in real time, set budgets, and establish limits on employees' token spending. It
expected to manage AI tokens "in a more structured way" in 2027, "with budgets,
allocation decisions, and supporting tools." The Information reported that a
copy of an internal leaderboard it reviewed in April showed employees had
consumed 60.2 trillion tokens in a 30-day period. The publication reported that
usage later reached 73.7 trillion tokens before the leaderboard was taken down."
