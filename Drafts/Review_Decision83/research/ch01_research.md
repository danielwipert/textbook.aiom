## Verified (read)

V1. [supports] Total consumption rises with the volume of work; a successful deployment overruns a budget set at the access price (sections 1.2, 1.4).
Source: Uber Technologies, Q1 2026 earnings call, FactSet corrected transcript, May 6, 2026, Uber IR: https://s23.q4cdn.com/407969754/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/Uber-Q1-26-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf
Exact words: analyst (John Colantuoni, Jefferies): "AI spending, where you already bumped up on your original full-year budget not long after the first quarter ended." CFO Balaji Krishnamurthy: "I would say candidly, when we set our budgets for 2026 in November, we underestimated the amount of impact the AI tools could have. And obviously in December we had new models come in. So we've re-upped our investment here. And as Dara said, we are trading that off against incremental head count growth". CEO Dara Khosrowshahi: "It means that our investment in AI tools and infrastructure is increasing. That will be offset by slower head count growth."

V2. [supports] Once use reaches scale, the buyer puts a meter and a control in place.
Source: Bloomberg News (Natalie Lung), "Uber Caps Usage of AI Tools Like Claude Code to Manage Costs (2)", June 2, 2026, Bloomberg Law syndication: https://news.bloomberglaw.com/artificial-intelligence/uber-caps-usage-of-ai-tools-like-claude-code-to-cut-costs-1
Exact words: "Uber Technologies Inc. has set usage caps on some artificial intelligence-powered tools used by its staff, a move meant to manage costs after the company blew through its AI budget earlier this year." "The rideshare giant is limiting all employees to $1,500 in monthly token spending per AI coding tool, an Uber spokesperson said in response to a Bloomberg News inquiry." "The limits, which have been instituted in recent months, only apply to agentic coding software such as Cursor or Anthropic PBC's Claude Code."
Correction to K4: the dashboard and exceptions process were not in the visible text.

V3. [supports] The bill rises with use at scale; a large buyer manages consumption, not seats.
Source (first party): Andy Markus, Chief Data and AI Officer, AT&T, "The tokenomics equation", AT&T blog, July 23, 2026: https://www.att.com/es-us/sdabout/blogs/2026/the-tokenomics-equation.html
Exact words: "Running advanced AI models can be expensive, especially at AT&T's scale: an average of 45 billion tokens per day." "we're using a proprietary cache-aware router to select the most cost-effective models for each task" "The result: we're reducing AI costs as much as 90%, already saving millions."
Second source: New York Times (Eli Tan), "Corporate America Is Getting Hooked on Open-Source AI", September 4, 2026, https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/04/technology/open-source-ai-anthropic-openai.html, read through a licensed reprint (The Star): "As AI costs skyrocketed, Andy Markus, the company's chief data and AI officer, looked for cheaper alternatives." "By May, open models accounted for 20% of AT&T's AI use." "That has since risen to 40% and may jump to 60% in the coming months, Markus said in an interview." "AT&T was saving up to 80% on AI costs compared with earlier this year."
Correction to K1 to K3: the AT&T blog gives no open-model share; "as much as 90%" (blog) and "up to 80%" (NYT) have different scopes.

V4. [supports] Each use creates an underlying cost that reaches the financial statements.
Source (first party, SEC): Duolingo, Q1 2025 shareholder letter (8-K): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000098/q1fy25duolingo3-31x25share.htm ; Q4 2024 letter: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000156208825000039/q4fy24duolingo12-31x24shar.htm ; FY2025 10-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1562088/000162828026012494/duol-20251231.htm
Exact words: Q1 2025: "even as it comes with variable costs that we are actively managing." "The year-over-year decline was mainly due to lower subscription gross margin as a result of increased generative AI costs related to the expansion of our Duolingo Max tier." Q4 2024: "gross margin decreased by approximately 120 basis points year over year to 71.9% due to lower subscription margins from increased generative AI costs due to the increased adoption of Duolingo Max". 10-K: "a decline in subscription gross margin, reflecting increased AI costs used in features like Video Call".
Caveat: a product-embedding buyer absorbing the meter behind its own flat subscription.

Not seen: "billions", 73.7 trillion tokens, AI Gateway, the Bosworth quote (pull list P1).

V6. [supports, aggregate, weaker] Spend tracks consumption.
Source (first party): Vercel, "AI Gateway Production Index", July 13, 2026: https://vercel.com/blog/ai-gateway-production-index-july-2026

V7. [cuts against, narrow] Flat prices hide a meter; the provider eventually ties what the buyer gets to consumption.
Source (first party): US General Services Administration, August 6, 2025: https://www.gsa.gov/about-gsa/newsroom/news-releases/gsa-announces-new-partnership-with-openai-delivering-deep-discount-to-chatgpt-08062025
