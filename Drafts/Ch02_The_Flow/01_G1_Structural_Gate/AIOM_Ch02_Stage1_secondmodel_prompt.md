# Stage 1 structural check: AI Operations Management, Chapter 2

**You are the independent check on a structural review.** Read the four files and
answer the questions below.

- **`AIOM_Ch02_prose_for_review.md`** is the chapter.
- **`AIOM_Ch02_Stage1_findings.md`** is the review you are checking. **Read it
  LAST**, after you have formed your own view.
- **`ch02_structure_extract.md`** and **`ch02_competencies_extract.md`** are the two
  documents the chapter must be measured against.

## Why you are being asked

This chapter was drafted by the same model that then reviewed it. **A chapter
judged by the model that drafted it is self-marking**, and this project has already
paid for that: a bias review of the same chapter found a structural defect, that
its diagnostic failed a test the chapter's own section 2.2 sets, which the drafting
model had not seen in its own work.

You are what replaces the human who used to run this step. **Your job is to find
what the drafter could not see about its own chapter.**

## The one question this step asks

**Is this the right chapter?** Not whether it is true, and not whether it is well
written. Those belong to other steps and findings about them will be discarded.

Concretely:

1. **Does the chapter DELIVER its two exit competencies, or only discuss them?**
   C2 is that a reader can identify the three flows in any real deployment and
   diagnose which are unmanaged. C3 is that a reader can state the central
   asymmetry and derive its consequence. **For each, quote the WEAKEST evidence in
   the chapter that it is delivered, and rule whether that evidence is enough.**
   Check the specified assessments in the competency document, not just the
   competency statements.
2. **Does the structure match what `ch02_structure_extract.md` specifies** for the
   big idea, the anchor theorem, and the craft section?
3. **Is any of this material another chapter's?** The extract lists the chapters
   before and after. Flag anything that spends a later chapter's content.
4. **Is the order right?** Eight teaching sections is a lot. If any section could be
   cut with no loss to the two competencies, name it.
5. **What is structurally missing** that a chapter with this big idea must contain?

## Then, and only then, read the review you are checking

`AIOM_Ch02_Stage1_findings.md` carries five findings, S1-1 through S1-5.

6. **Which of its findings do you disagree with, and why?**
7. **What did it miss?** This is the question you were brought in for. The drafting
   model wrote both the chapter and that review.
8. **Is any finding overstated** in a way that would cause unnecessary rework?

## Constraints

- Quote the chapter for every finding. An unquoted finding cannot be acted on.
- Rank by severity. Say plainly if a section is sound.
- No line edits, no wording suggestions, no fact checking. All three will be
  discarded, and every figure in the chapter is already known to be unverified.
- Structural findings can be expensive to fix. Say what is wrong, not how to write
  the replacement.
