# Chapter 2, Stage 7. External check B: the text against itself.

**Give this prompt and `AIOM_Ch02_Stage7_render.pdf` to a DIFFERENT model, in its own
session. Give it NO packet, NO register, NO source notes, and NOT check A's output.**

**The missing packet is deliberate and is the whole design of this check.** Check A is
reading the sources. You are reading the chapter as a sceptical, well-informed reader
receives it, with nothing but what is on the page. A claim that only survives because
the reader trusts a footnote they cannot see is the failure this check exists to find.

---

You are reading one chapter of an academic textbook before it locks. You have the
chapter and nothing else. Do not look anything up, and do not tell us whether its
facts are true: another checker is doing that. **Your question is different. Does this
chapter claim more than it has shown?**

## What to look for

**1. Sentences asserting more than the chapter elsewhere admits it knows.** This
chapter is unusually explicit about the limits of its own evidence, and it has a
section that states what it does not claim. **Find every place where the confident
sentence and the careful sentence disagree**, and quote both. A chapter that hedges in
section 2.8 and asserts flatly in the opening case has a problem the careful section
does not fix.

**2. Attribution that appears and disappears.** Some claims are attributed in the
sentence that carries them ("press reports indicated"), some are asserted flat. **Is
the pattern principled or accidental?** Name any flat assertion that carries the same
epistemic weight as a neighbouring attributed one. Note that a footnote marker is not
an answer here: you are judging what the sentence itself tells the reader.

**3. Quantities and their scope.** The chapter's own subject is the mismatch between a
number and the scope it covers. **Turn that on the chapter.** For every figure, range,
proportion and count on the page, ask what population it covers, whether the sentence
says so, and whether a later sentence reuses it as though it covered something
broader. Include ranges given in words rather than digits.

**4. A named product, a named company, a named executive.** The chapter names a
company, its product, and an executive with a title and a direct quotation. **Check
every one of those for internal consistency across the whole chapter**, including the
craft section and the problems, which are far from the opening case. A title given two
ways, a product named in one place and called "the tool" in another, or a quotation
whose surrounding paraphrase says something slightly different from the quotation
itself.

**5. The worked example and the four problems.** Do they rest on facts the chapter
established, on facts it stipulated, or on facts it neither established nor labelled
as stipulated? **The third category is what to report.** Say for each which it is.

**6. Anything a reader could reasonably read as a claim the chapter would not defend.**
Especially any statement about what organizations generally, usually, commonly or
typically do. **This chapter has withdrawn several such claims already and reintroduced
two of them by accident.** Quote every one you find, whatever form it takes, including
ones carrying no frequency word at all but implying frequency through a generic plural.

## Rules for your findings

- **Number every finding.** Quote the exact sentence and give its section.
- **Say what a reader would wrongly conclude**, not merely that the sentence is loose.
  A finding without a wrong conclusion attached is a style note, and style is not this
  step.
- **Rank your findings** and say which single one you would fix if you could fix only
  one.
- **A proposed remedy is welcome and will be judged separately from the finding.** Two
  house rules constrain remedies and a remedy breaking either will be rejected while
  the finding stands: body prose carries **no hedging language** ("perhaps", "some
  argue", "one might say"), and **no em dashes anywhere**. Making a sentence vaguer is
  not an acceptable fix; making it narrower is.
- **Do not comment on prose quality, rhythm, structure, or teaching.** Other steps own
  those and have closed.
- **Ignore the placement of footnote 5.** It is a known pagination defect booked to
  the design review.
