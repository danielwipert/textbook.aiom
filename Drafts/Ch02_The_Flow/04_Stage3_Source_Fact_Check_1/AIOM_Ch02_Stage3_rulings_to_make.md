# Chapter 2, Stage 3. The five uncited claims, drafted for ruling

Built 2026-08-29. **Nothing here is applied.** Each item carries the text as it
stands, the options standing rule 2 allows, and a recommendation. Dan rules.

**THE POINT OF THIS SHEET IS THAT NONE OF THE FIVE NEEDS A SOURCE.** Standing rule
2 allows citation, recasting as a formal conditional, or cutting. Three of these
five can be recast onto derivations the chapter already contains and two can be cut
without losing teaching, so the whole set can be ruled from the chapter itself.
That leaves Stage 3's genuinely external work at the five register entries alone.

---

## 1. Section 2.4, the decay illustration

> The decay is worth one concrete illustration, because managers routinely
> underestimate it.

The frequency claim is "managers routinely underestimate it". It justifies
including an example rather than carrying argument.

- **CUT (recommended).** "The decay is worth one concrete illustration." The
  illustration that follows earns the point without being told to, and the excised
  clause is close to the signposting standing rule 5 rules out.
- **RECAST.** Anchor it to the mechanism the same paragraph derives, that the
  context stops being recoverable without anything announcing it.
- **CITE.** Needs a study of manager estimation error. Nothing in the register
  reaches it.

## 2. Section 2.7, the three parts of cost governance

> Most organizations that believe they have cost governance have the first,
> sometimes the second, and rarely the third.

**This one carries argumentative weight**, so cutting costs teaching. It sets up
the dashboard sentence that follows.

- **RECAST (recommended).** Move the claim from how often to what is separable:

  > The three are separable, and an organization can hold the first without the
  > second and the first two without the third. Where the record exists and the
  > constraint does not, a dashboard nobody acts on converts a surprise into a
  > slower surprise, which is an improvement, and it is not control.

  Every teaching function survives and the population claim goes.
- **CITE.** Needs a survey of cost-governance maturity. Nothing in the register
  reaches it, and finding one is real external work.
- **CUT.** Not recommended. The paragraph loses its hinge.

## 3. Craft section, the common result

> Organizations are usually surprised by the second finding rather than the third,
> because the absence of a record is invisible until somebody asks a question that
> needs one.

**The sentence already contains its own derivation.** The clause after "because"
is a mechanism, not a report of frequency, so the unsourced half is only the
wrapper.

- **RECAST (recommended).** "The second finding surprises where the third does
  not, because the absence of a record is invisible until somebody asks a question
  that needs one." The mechanism is unchanged and "Organizations are usually" goes.
- **CUT.** Loses a real teaching point about which gap gets noticed.

## 4. Craft section, the forward reference to Chapter 8

> the record flow built there is the flow this mapping most often finds missing

- **RECAST (recommended), and it is clean by an argument already accepted.**
  Section 2.5 is titled "Why the record flow is the one that gets skipped" and
  derives it. Stage 1 ruled a SIXTH candidate clean, "the record flow is skipped
  most often, and for structural reasons rather than careless ones", **on exactly
  the ground that 2.5 derives it.** Anchoring this sentence to the same derivation
  makes it clean by the same reasoning:

  > the record flow built there is the one section 2.5 shows is structurally the
  > easiest to skip

- **CUT.** Available, and it costs the forward reference Chapter 8 pays.

## 5. Problem P3, the DTA trial

> This organization commissioned an independent evaluation, published it, and let
> it report the limits of its own method, which is more than most buyers do.

- **CUT (recommended).** The sentence before it already distinguishes this case
  from the opening case, so the trailing clause adds a population claim and little
  else. Cutting also ends the sentence on the load-bearing clause, which is C5.
- **RECAST.** Compare against the opening case's buyer specifically rather than
  against buyers in general, which converts a frequency claim into a concrete
  particular. It changes the meaning and should be ruled on its merits.
