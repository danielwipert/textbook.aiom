# Northmoor Dataset: Construction Note

All figures in this book that carry the Northmoor name are synthetic. They come
from a single seeded generator (northmoor_gen.py, seed 20260723). The generator
fixes the company structure, applies seasonal demand curves, and layers in three
engineered anomalies during the overspend quarter. Six pedagogical properties
(attribution reordering, the 62% quarter, seasonal forecastability, routing
economics, the value netting, and compressibility) are asserted by automated
checks before any table is written, so the exercises are guaranteed solvable and
mutually consistent.

Northmoor Apparel is a fictional entity. Any resemblance to a real company,
trade name, or logo is coincidental. Run a trade-name collision check before
print. The workflows, providers, and dollar figures are illustrative and are not
drawn from any real firm's records.

Token prices are stylized round numbers on a three-tier ladder and are not quoted
from any provider's list price. They are chosen to make the routing and
attribution lessons legible, not to track a market.
